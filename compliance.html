Candidate actions

What still needs a real person or internal access.

  1. Required: provide complete actual AI interactions for every contributing session, including this revision and Claude Cowork. Identify genuine gaps; do not substitute recreated turns. The manifest lists known material.
  2. Required timing check: confirm the assignment receipt time, deadline and submission destination. This workflow publishes the artifact; it does not send a message to the hiring panel.
  3. Candidate authorship: add a short note in your own words about judgments you personally made. The AI-authored log must not be passed off as a personal interview or decision record.
  4. Evidence improvement: conduct the proposed purchase-reconstruction conversations and an authenticated baseline walkthrough if access/time permits. Preserve contrary findings. Neither has been fabricated here.
  5. Optional named attribution: the current release omits the stakeholder identity and treats the role as unverified. Only restore it after verifying role and obtaining permission. Public Git history may retain the earlier version.

Internal cohort data, policy approval, private commission, partner permission and live service access are product-validation dependencies. They are not numbers or contracts the candidate should invent to make the assignment look complete.
