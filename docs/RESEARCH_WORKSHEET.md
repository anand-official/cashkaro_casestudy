# Purchase-reconstruction research worksheet

Status: **not conducted**. Proposed 5–8 existing CashKaro users, including repeat users and people whose first transaction was followed by a long gap. This sample is for mechanism discovery, not prevalence estimation. Obtain permission before recording; anonymize notes. Do not contact people automatically.

For each person's last three eligible online purchases, reconstruct what happened before showing the concept:

1. What did you buy, when, on which device and retailer? How did you first reach that retailer?
2. At what point, if any, did CashKaro come to mind? Show the route if comfortable; do not request account credentials.
3. What made you use or skip it on that purchase? Ask for the actual sequence before offering explanations.
4. What reward did you expect, when did you expect it, and could you spend or withdraw it? What happened?
5. Did you look for tracking or contact support? What did you understand then?
6. What did you do on the next purchase? Could a clearer previous order have changed that route, or was the barrier something else?
7. Give the participant the current journey and the proposed receipt in alternating order. Ask them to identify whether there is a purchase, whether money is withdrawable, and the next valid step. Observe rather than explain.
8. Ask what would make them ignore or disable the receipt. A positive opinion is not evidence of repeat routing.
