## The retained disagreement

The universal proposal is the strategic choice because it tests whether an existing rewards/routing business can participate in external purchase surfaces without owning discovery. Share is a credible lower-risk implementation surface. It is not automatically the best long-term distribution answer. The decision is defensible only with a bounded investment and willingness to kill AI expansion if reach, invocation or economics fail.
