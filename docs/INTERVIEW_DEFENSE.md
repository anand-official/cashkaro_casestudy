# Explain the decision in your own words

These are AI-assisted preparation notes, not a fabricated candidate statement.

**Thirty seconds:** Existing CashKaro users can finish a shopping decision elsewhere and then need to remember to restart through CashKaro. I propose making CashKaro callable at that decision: the assistant owns the recommendation, CashKaro checks the benefit and generates an approved route, and the retailer completes the sale. One Purchase Router can support different hosts. I would test a narrow existing-user cohort and count all-channel orders, including people who never connect.

**Why this rather than Share?** Share is more controllable today but still requires recall. The strategic bet is that a host can offer the skill at purchase intent. If it cannot, I would reduce or stop the AI investment; the router may still serve Share.

**Why not own discovery?** CashKaro's relevant advantage is benefit eligibility, attribution and reward fulfillment. A new recommendation destination must win a harder habit and data battle.

**Who pays and why?** The retailer pays affiliate commission under an approved agreement. V1 may recapture attribution and benefit the shopper without creating retailer demand. Consent does not remove the partner's right to reject or reprice that traffic.

**Isn't universal misleading?** It describes a reusable CashKaro service, not universal installation, identical UI or guaranteed access to every assistant.

**Why is a phone demo acceptable for an order-frequency assignment?** It makes the handoff easy to understand. The pilot needs enough eligible repeat opportunities across supported categories; a rare phone purchase alone is insufficient.

**What did evidence establish?** The brief establishes bypass. The public route establishes re-entry. One inherited respondent supports friction/value/trust hypotheses. Platform and survey evidence establish emerging external research capability, not CashKaro demand or uplift.

**What kills it?** Inadequate cohort reach, unusable invocation, rejected traffic, unreliable context/benefit/attribution, insufficient user value, channel-only lift, poor contribution or native platform incentives that remove CashKaro's role.

**What does the prototype actually do?** It runs fictional local scenarios and validates state transitions. It creates no real account, API integration, affiliate link or order.

**What is missing?** Real purchase reconstruction, internal reach and economics, authenticated baseline, platform/partner approval and complete AI exports. These are disclosed rather than fabricated.

Before interviewing, answer personally: which trade-off would you still choose if every AI disagreed, and what observed evidence would reverse it? Do not memorize these notes as a substitute for understanding.
